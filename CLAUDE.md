# CK Products Ordering Portal — Working Rules

## Workflow
1. First, think through the problem. Read the codebase and write a plan in `tasks/todo.md`.
2. The plan should be a checklist of todo items.
3. Check in with the user before starting work — they'll verify the plan.
4. Then, complete the todos one by one, marking them off as you go.
5. At every step, give a high-level explanation of what changed.
6. Keep every change minimal and simple. Avoid big rewrites.
7. At the end, add a review section in `tasks/todo.md` summarizing the changes.
